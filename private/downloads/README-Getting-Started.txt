GETTING STARTED
1. Open the Excel workbook and save your own working copy.
2. Start in Settings and replace the example business profile with your own details.
3. Add your services or products in Services.
4. Add leads and clients, then create a quotation in Quotations.
5. Add one or more detail rows in Quote Line Items using the same Quote ID.
6. Create an invoice linked to the Quote ID and record receipts in Payments.
7. Review the Dashboard and Monthly Report.

IMPORTANT
This is a blank-use edition. No demo customer or transaction records are included.
Use one currency consistently. Verify local tax and invoice requirements independently.
This is an operational spreadsheet, not accounting, tax, legal or regulatory advice.
